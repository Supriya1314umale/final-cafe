High-Protein Salad Bowls

Fresh lettuce, cucumber, tomatoes, sprouts, quinoa, boiled chickpeas or grilled Chicken/paneer, topped with light olive-oil dressing.

Chicken/Paneer Meal Boxes

Steamed rice or millet, grilled chicken or paneer cubes, sautéed veggies, light spices, and a high-protein gravy or dip.

Egg Protein Meals

Boiled or scrambled eggs, whole-wheat toast or rice, mixed veggies, herbs, and minimal oil for a clean protein-packed dish.

Fresh fruits, low-fat milk or yogurt, oats or nuts, whey/plant protein powder, blended smooth with no added sugar.
